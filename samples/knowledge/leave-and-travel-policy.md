# Leave and travel policy (sample)

## Casual leave

Every employee gets 12 days of casual leave in a calendar year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward. Unused casual leave lapses on 31 December.

Casual leave of more than 3 days in a row needs approval from the reporting manager at least 5 working days ahead.

## Earned leave

Earned leave builds up at 1.5 days for every month worked. Up to 30 days of earned leave can be carried forward to the next year. Earned leave above 30 days is paid out in the April salary.

## Sick leave

Employees get 8 days of sick leave a year. Sick leave of more than 2 days in a row needs a medical certificate.

## Travel booking

Book flights at least 7 days before the travel date. Bookings made later need approval from the department head.

Hotel stays are capped at 6000 rupees a night in metro cities and 4000 rupees a night in other cities.

## Expense claims

Submit expense claims within 15 days of the end of the trip. Claims need the original bill for any single expense above 500 rupees. Approved claims are paid with the next salary run.
